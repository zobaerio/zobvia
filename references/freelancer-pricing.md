# Freelancer Pricing Guidance

> **Guidance only — not guarantees.** These are working market ranges to quote from, observed for South-Asian freelance video editing sold locally and internationally. Your rate depends on portfolio, speed, niche and client. Adjust; don't treat any figure here as a promised price or income.

## Bangladesh Market (BDT)

| Work | Typical range (BDT) |
|---|---|
| Short-form edit (Reels/Shorts/TikTok, captions + SFX, ≤60 s) | ৳500–2,500 per video |
| Short-form monthly pack (12–20 videos) | ৳8,000–30,000 / month |
| YouTube long-form edit (8–15 min, captions + graphics) | ৳2,000–8,000 per video |
| Motion-graphics video (Remotion-style, per finished minute) | ৳3,000–10,000 / min |

## Packages (structure, price from tables above)

- **Basic:** edit + burned captions (1 preset) + SFX pass, 1 revision round, 1 platform export.
- **Standard:** Basic + motion graphics/cards, hook rewrite, 2 revision rounds, 2 platform exports + SRT sidecar.
- **Premium:** Standard + thumbnail, multi-aspect exports (16:9 + 9:16 + 1:1), priority turnaround, 3 revision rounds, delivery ZIP + credits file.

Charge extra, quoted upfront, for: rush (<24 h), extra revision rounds, VO recording/TTS, scriptwriting, re-edits after final delivery, source-project handover.

## Terms That Protect You

- **50% advance before work starts** is the norm for new clients; balance on preview approval, before final unwatermarked delivery. Long-term clients can move to monthly invoicing — after trust exists.
- Preview renders may carry a watermark/timecode until balance is paid, if stated in the agreement upfront.
- **Revisions:** a revision = changes to the agreed brief/script, listed in one message. New script, new topic or new footage after approval = new job or a quoted extra.
- State delivery format, resolution and round count in writing (the brief form does this) before the advance.
- Keep the Remotion project/SRT until final payment clears; deliver source files only if the package includes them.

## Red Flags

- "Pay after it goes viral" / exposure-only pay.
- No advance from a new client + rush deadline + vague brief.
- Client insists on ripped commercial music or pirated assets (see `music-sfx-sourcing.md`).
- Endless "one small change" outside the revision rounds.
- Requests to remove watermarks/credits from others' work, or to fake results/testimonials in the edit.
