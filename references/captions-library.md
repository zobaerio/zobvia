## Universal Rules

- Reading speed: keep a line on screen ≥1.2 s; ≤20 characters/second (Latin), ≤16 cps for Bengali/Arabic.
- Contrast: white text needs stroke/shadow on bright footage; test on the brightest frame, not the average frame.
- One preset per video. Hooks may use a bigger variant of the *same* preset, not a different one.
- Punctuation: keep sentence punctuation; drop trailing periods on pop/karaoke presets.
- Speaker change = new caption block, even mid-sentence.
- Never auto-break SRT lines by character count alone — break at phrase boundaries.
