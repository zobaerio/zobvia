# CTA & Outro Library — 12 Patterns

One CTA per video. Two max on long-form (one mid, one end). A CTA that asks for everything gets nothing.

| # | Pattern | Timing | Layout | Wording (EN / BN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subscribe Reminder | At a value peak, ~70% through — not only at the end | Small lower-corner animation, 3–4 s, no full-screen stop | "If this helped, subscribe — I post one like this every week." / "ভালো লাগলে সাবস্ক্রাইব করুন — প্রতি সপ্তাহে এমন ভিডিও আসে।" |
| 2 | Next-Video End Screen | Final 10–20 s (YouTube) | End-screen element right/left, host points to it, keep area uncluttered | "Watch this one next — it fixes the next step." |
| 3 | Comment Question | End, or right after the key point | Big on-screen question text, pinned comment matches | "Which one do you use — A or B? Tell me below." / "আপনি কোনটা ব্যবহার করেন? কমেন্টে জানান।" |
| 4 | Lead Magnet | After delivering the promised value | QR/link graphic + verbal URL, 5–7 s | "The free checklist is linked below — grab it before you start." |
| 5 | Product / Service Link | Mid-roll soft mention + end card | Product shot, price honesty, link in description | "I used [tool] for this — link below, it's what I actually use." Only for real use; disclose sponsorship. |
| 6 | Playlist Binge | End screen | Playlist card + "start from episode 1" text | "This is part 3 — the full series is in this playlist." |
| 7 | Community Post Tie-In | End, 5 s | Screenshot-style graphic of the poll/post | "I put a poll on the community tab — your vote picks the next video." |
| 8 | Soft Brand Outro | Final 3–5 s | Logo + brand colour + one-line tagline, music tail | No words needed beyond tagline. For corporate/client work only — not creator Shorts. |
| 9 | Loop-Back-to-Hook | Final seconds (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) | Last line verbally + visually connects to frame 1; no outro card | End mid-thought that resolves into the opening line, so the loop replays seamlessly. |
| 10 | Bengali Creator CTA Set | End 5–8 s | BN text large, one action only | "লাইক দিন, কমেন্ট করুন, আর বন্ধুদের সাথে শেয়ার করুন।" Pick ONE of the three per video — rotate across uploads. |
| 11 | Save / Share Prompt | Right after a list, recipe or tutorial step | Bookmark/share icon animation | "Save this — you'll need it when you [do the task]." Saves/shares outrank likes on Reels/TikTok. |
| 12 | Live / Event Invite | End + description | Date/time graphic in viewer's timezone terms, calendar note | "Live Q&A this Friday 8 pm — bring your questions." Only for a real scheduled event. |

## Outro Rules

- Never end on a black frame, a fade to silence, or "that's all for today, bye" with dead air — end on the CTA or loop point.
- End screens (YouTube) need 5–20 s of usable tail: plan the shot, keep captions clear of the element zones (corners/centre-right).
- Shorts/Reels/TikTok: no end card at all — the CTA is spoken + on-screen text in the last 2–3 s, or the loop-back.
- Say the benefit, not just the action: "Subscribe for weekly BOQ tips" beats "Please subscribe".
- Client work: CTA wording, links and handles come from the brief (`client-brief-template.md`) — never invent a handle, price or offer.
