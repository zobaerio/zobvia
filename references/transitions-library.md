# Transitions Library — 18 Transitions

Durations in frames at 30 fps (scale ×2 at 60 fps). Energy: L/M/H. A transition must motivate a cut — time, place, topic or energy change — not decorate every cut. Default is still a hard cut.

| # | Transition | Frames | Energy | Use when | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hard Cut (baseline) | 0 | any | Same scene, dialogue, pace is fast | Never wrong; overusing effects is the error |
| 2 | Whip Pan | 8–12 | H | Location/topic jump, vlog, travel, product | Interviews, serious/news, more than 3×/video |
| 3 | Zoom-Blur (punch transition) | 6–10 | H | Into detail, reveal, beat drop | Low-res source (blur exposes it) |
| 4 | Mask Wipe | 12–18 | M | Object/person passes frame; seamless scene change | No clean passing object — don't fake it badly |
| 5 | Luma Fade | 10–15 | M | Bright/dark matched shots, dreamy or tech feel | Dialogue cuts |
| 6 | Glitch Cut | 5–8 | H | Tech, gaming, error/fail moments, pattern interrupt | Corporate, Islamic, news, wedding |
| 7 | Spin | 10–14 | H | Playful reveal, before/after, kids/fashion | Anything solemn; motion-sickness risk if repeated |
| 8 | Slide Push | 10–15 | M | List items, comparisons, course lessons, chapters | Emotional/story beats |
| 9 | Morph Cut | 6–12 | M | Same framing, jump-cut cleanup (talking head) | Different angles/lighting — morph warps visibly |
| 10 | Dip-to-Colour | 12–20 | M | Chapter change; dip to brand colour, not always black | Back-to-back segments (slows pace) |
| 11 | Light Leak | 15–25 | L | Warm/nostalgic shift, wedding, travel, golden hour | Tech/corporate; fake leaks on night footage |
| 12 | Film Burn | 12–20 | M | Retro segment change, music, memory/flashback | News, finance, anything trust-led |
| 13 | Shutter / Strobe Cut | 4–6 | H | Beat-synced montage, sport, fashion | Photosensitive risk: never strobe >3 flashes/sec |
| 14 | Split Reveal | 12–18 | M | Before/after, comparison, two locations | Single-subject simple cuts |
| 15 | Circle Iris | 12–18 | M | Focus onto a detail, playful chapter end, kids | Serious tone; overuse reads dated |
| 16 | Seamless Object Pass | 8–15 | H | Camera "passes through" a door/hand/screen into next scene | No matching object in both shots |
| 17 | Cross Dissolve | 15–30 | L | Time passing, same-location lapse, emotional soften | Fast formats — dissolves kill Shorts pacing |
| 18 | J/L Cut (audio-led) | audio ±6–15 | L | Dialogue/interview flow: next voice or ambience starts before/after picture | Not a visual effect — don't stack visuals on it |

## Pairing Rules

- SFX pairs: whip pan → whoosh; zoom-blur → riser + impact; glitch → digital glitch SFX (short, −6 dB under voice); dip → soft fade, no SFX; object pass → whoosh + room-tone change.
- Max 2 *different* effect transitions per 60 s of short-form; long-form: one family per video.
- Match direction: pan/slide exits left → next shot enters from left. Direction flips feel like mistakes.
- Transitions never cover a caption's key word or the first word of a new sentence.
