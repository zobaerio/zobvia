# Hooks Library — 18 First-3-Seconds Patterns

Rule: the hook is frame 1, not second 3. No intro, logo, greeting or fade-in before it. On-screen text + spoken line say the *same* promise in different words.

| # | Pattern | On-screen text formula | Audio / SFX note | Example line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bold Claim | `[Big result] — without [pain]` | Impact hit on frame 1, voice starts frame 0 | "I edited this whole video without a laptop." |
| 2 | Open Question | `Why does [X] always [fail]?` | Rising tone / soft riser | "Why do your videos look cheap even in 4K?" |
| 3 | Mistake Myth | `Stop doing [common thing]!` | Record-scratch or stop SFX | "Stop adding music under your voice — do this instead." |
| 4 | Before/After Tease | Split screen: `BEFORE → AFTER` | Whoosh into the "after" | Show the finished result for 1 s, then rewind: "Here's how." |
| 5 | Number Promise | `[3/5/7] [things] that [benefit]` | Pop SFX per number | "5 caption mistakes killing your views." |
| 6 | Pattern Interrupt Zoom | Unexpected word, huge: `WAIT.` / snap zoom | Snap zoom + impact, 0.3 s silence after | Freeze, zoom 140%: "— that was the mistake." |
| 7 | Headline News Style | News lower-third: `[BREAKING-style headline]` | News sting (licensed/original only) | "New rule: YouTube now pushes this format." |
| 8 | Story Cold-Open | Mid-action frame + `It started when…` | Ambience first, no music for 2 s | Open on the climax moment, no context: "…and then the roof leaked." |
| 9 | Tool Reveal | Object/product centre frame, name big | Shutter/unbox SFX | Hold the tool up: "This ৳500 light beats my old setup." |
| 10 | Result First | Final result full-screen, `Final result ↓` | Beat drop on reveal | Show the finished house/dish/design first, process after. |
| 11 | Challenge | `Can I [hard thing] in [time]?` + countdown graphic | Timer tick under voice | "Can I finish this BOQ in 10 minutes?" |
| 12 | Secret / Insider | `Nobody tells you this about [X]` | Lower music, conspiratorial close voice | "Editors don't want you to know this shortcut." |
| 13 | Comparison | `A vs B` split, prices/labels visible | Two pops, one per side | "Free vs paid captions — watch the difference." |
| 14 | Countdown | Big `3…2…1` into the payoff frame | Tick + impact on 1 | Only when the payoff is genuinely immediate. |
| 15 | Direct Address | `You.` + specific situation | Silence, then voice close to mic | "You — yes, you posting without captions. Watch this." |
| 16 | Loss Warning | `You're losing [money/time] because…` | Low impact, serious tone | "You're losing clients with this one invoice mistake." |
| 17 | Bengali Curiosity (BN) | Big BN text: `এটা জানলে অবাক হবেন` style — specific, not clickbait | Pop + voice in BN from frame 0 | "এই একটা ভুলের কারণে আপনার ভিডিও ভাইরাল হচ্ছে না।" |
| 18 | Proof Flash | Screenshot/statistic full-screen for 1.5 s | Shutter SFX | Show the number/result first: "0 to 12,000 subscribers — the change was this." |

## Hook Rules

- One hook per video. Stacking claim + question + countdown dilutes all three.
- The hook makes a promise the video must pay off — note the payoff timestamp while scripting; if there's no payoff, rewrite the hook.
- Captions on from frame 1, in the hook's own big style (see `captions-library.md` presets 3/13).
- First spoken word within 0.5 s. Silence, music-only or "Assalamu alaikum / hello guys welcome back" openings are not hooks — greeting goes *after* the hook if at all.
- Test: mute + cover the audio — does frame 1 alone make you want to continue? If not, rework frame 1.
